Galaxy Map Normandy, a mod for Mass Effect 1 in the Legendary Edition

On the galaxy map, a lit SR-1 Normandy takes the place of the orange cursor
ring, as in Mass Effect 2 and 3. The mouse works as in Mass Effect 2, with its
Enter Orbit button, and the controller works as before.

You need:
- Mass Effect Legendary Edition
- the LE1 Community Patch 2.0 or later
- ME3Tweaks Mod Manager (M3)

Install:
1. Close the game.
2. Drop this 7z file on the M3 window. Or use Mod Management > Import Mod >
   Import mod from .7z/.rar/.zip/.exe/.me2mod and pick the file.
   Click Import mods.
3. Select Galaxy Map Normandy in the mod library and click Apply Mod.

Uninstall: close the game. In M3, select your LE1 game, click Manage Target
to open Installation Information, and remove DLC_MOD_GalaxyMapNormandy from
the list of installed DLC mods.

Settings: pause the game, open Mod Settings and pick Galaxy Map Normandy. You
can set the ship's speed, turn rate, response and size.
